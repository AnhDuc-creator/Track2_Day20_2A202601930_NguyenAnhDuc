--- Teaching notes (Day 20 §5 Serving Regimes) ---
 * Embedding serving is PREFILL-BOUND: 1 forward pass/text, no KV cache, no decode loop.
 * Throughput comes from large STATIC batches (token-sorted), not continuous batching.
 * FP8 gives ~50% more throughput at >99% cosine similarity (Snowflake Arctic: 16x vLLM).
 * RAG/agent inference is HALF retrieval — this is the other serving discipline.
 * For real retrieval quality use a dedicated embedding model (Qwen3-Embedding, BGE-M3);
   reusing a chat GGUF here keeps the demo to zero extra downloads.
